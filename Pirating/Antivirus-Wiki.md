# What is Antivirus Software:
- Most malicious programs detected by Antivirus Software are what is called Agents, Downloaders and Trojan Horses (Malware for short).
- The area of computer security is vast and contains: Anti-adware, anti-bootkit, anti-rootkit, anti-spyware, anti-trojan, endpoint detection and response, firewall, HIPS, internet security, security suites
