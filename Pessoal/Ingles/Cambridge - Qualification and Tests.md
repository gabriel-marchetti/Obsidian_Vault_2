# Qualifications and Tests

## Cambridge English Qualifications
**Pre A1 to A2 (Young Learners)**: First steps in learning English - focused in learning for childs.
**A2 Key**: Communicate at a basic level
**B1 Preliminary**: Intermediate-level qualification - mastery of the basics of English and the practical language use
**B2 First:** Control of the language skills needed to communicate
**C1 Advanced**: High-level achievement in English - Ideal qualification for university or professional life.
**C2 Proficiency**: Exceptional level of English

## Linguaskill - online certificated English exam
- It's range goes from B1 to C2
- It is an exam that tests your English - It seems to be used in exams.
- Modular - Speaking, Writing, Reading and Listening
- There is also Linguaskill Business

## IELTS - used mainly in migration and higher education
- It is just an exam

## Cambridge English Skill Test 
- Online, on-demand and modular test for all four english skills
