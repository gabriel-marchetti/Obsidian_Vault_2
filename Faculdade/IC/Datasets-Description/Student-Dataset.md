# Properties:
There are two completely different datasets contained here. They are "student-mat.csv" and "student-por.csv".
# Attribute Description:
Attribute 1 - Student School - Two options of school
Attribute 2 - Student's sex
Attribute 3 - Student's age
Attribute 4 - Student's address type - Rural or Urban
Attribute 5 - Family Size - LE3 (Less equal than 3) or GT3 (Greater than 3)
Attribute 6 - Parent cohabitation status
Attribute 7 - Mother Education 
Attribute 8 - Father Education
Attribute 9 - Mother Job - Selection of pre-established options 
Attribute 10 - Father Job - Selection of pre-established options
Attribute 11 - Reason to choose this school
Attribute 12 - Student Guardian
Attribute 13 - Travel time to school
Attribute 14 - Studytime
Attribute 15 - Number of past class failures (above 4 it does not increase)
Attribute 16 - Extra educational support - Binary
Attribute 17 - Family educational support - Binary
Attribute 18 - Extra paid classes for the course - Binary
Attribute 19 - Extra-curricular classes - Binary
Attribute 20 - Attended Nursery School - Binary
Attribute 21 - Wants to take higher education - Binary
Attribute 22 - Has access to internet at home - Binary 
Attribute 23 - Has romantic relation - Binary
Attribute 24 - Quality of family relation - from 1 (bad) to 5 (excellent)
Attribute 25 - Freetime after school - from 1 (little) to 5 (plenty)
Attribute 26 - Go out with friends - from 1 (little) to 5 (plenty)
Attribute 27 - Workday alcohol consumption - from 1 (little) to 5 (plenty) 
Attribute 28 - Weekend alcohol consumption - from 1 (little) to 5 (plenty)
Attribute 29 - Health Status - from 1 (bad) to 5 (good)
Attribute 30 - Number of School Absences

There seems to be three output values - G1, G2, G3 which corresponds to Grade1, Grade2, Grade3
# Points to Investigate:
This dataset has many potential problems with fairness like Attribute 2 (sex), Attribute 4 (Address Type) and so on.
